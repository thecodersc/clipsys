# Account: acct_01 — YouTube Shorts

## Voice
Energetic, enthusiastic, hype-driven. Short punchy sentences. Use caps for emphasis.
Target audience: GTA fans aged 16-28, English-speaking.

## Style rules
- Always start the hook with an action verb or a question.
- Keep title under 60 characters.
- End description with a call-to-action (like, subscribe).
- Use 3-5 relevant hashtags only. No spam.

## Banned phrases
- "you won't believe"
- "shocking"
- "jaw-dropping"
- "must watch"
- "insane trick" (use "wild move" instead)
- profanity
